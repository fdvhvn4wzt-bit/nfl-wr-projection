📊 Confidence Rating Guide

🟢 HIGH CONFIDENCE
Stronger statistical support. The player's performance has been relatively consistent.

🟡 MEDIUM CONFIDENCE
Moderate statistical support. Some variation in the player's recent performance.

🔴 LOW CONFIDENCE
Greater uncertainty due to limited data or inconsistent performance.

Remember: Confidence measures statistical support for the projection, not the probability of winning a bet.

NFL statistics via nflverse (postgame). Sportsbook lines require an Odds API key. Cached odds may be older than current sportsbook prices. Confidence labels are not win probabilities. Always verify lines before placing a bet.
